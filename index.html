The Exhibition

Meet China’s universities, booth by booth

On the afternoon of 20 October, Harokopio University of Athens welcomes you to the Chinese Universities Exhibition. Each of the 18 participating universities has its own booth, where its representatives are ready to greet visitors and give you a warm welcome.

Visit the booths

Walk through the exhibition and stop at the booths of 18 universities from across China.

Be welcomed in person

University representatives greet you at their booth. Introduce yourself, learn about their university and ask what you would like to know.

Take home a souvenir

The Chinese universities have prepared souvenirs for visitors, a small keepsake to remember your afternoon.
